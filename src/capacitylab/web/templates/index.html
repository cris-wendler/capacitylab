Review database capacity decisions with the evidence in front of everyone

A database engineer, an application owner, a reliability engineer, a cost (FinOps) analyst, and a representative of the affected tenant look at the same decision. Each sees the evidence their job gives them, asks for checks, and takes a position. The result is a written record of what each role recommends, what is still disputed, and what nobody has measured yet.

1

Evidence

Scenario data, measurements from the local MySQL lab, or exports from your own database. Every item says where it came from.

2

Review rounds

Roles cite evidence, request checks (capacity model, cost, index tests, rewrite checks, lab load tests), and challenge each other. Numbers they state must appear in what they cite.

3

Decision record

Final positions, open disagreements, missing evidence, and a log you can replay to verify every check.
