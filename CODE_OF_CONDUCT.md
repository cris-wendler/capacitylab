# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build a small tool well. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is
expected to be respectful, including when they disagree, to discuss the work rather than the person, to assume good
intent and ask when something is unclear, to accept that a maintainer may decline a change that is outside the scope,
and to leave a thread when it stops being useful.

Unacceptable: personal attacks, harassment of any kind, discriminatory language, publishing someone's private
information, and persistently reopening a decision that has been made.

A maintainer may edit, hide or delete a comment, close a thread, or block an account, and will say why when they do.

To report something, use the private contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than a public issue.
